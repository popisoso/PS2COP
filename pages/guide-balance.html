The Challenge

As an organization, you often try to organize activities and gatherings that are as accessible as possible for your target group. You invest a lot of time and energy in your organization, often on a voluntary basis. You usually want your activities or gatherings to be well attended. Ideally, you’d like visitors to eventually become active members of your organization. But how do you ensure that people stay engaged with your organization? How do you motivate people and make sure they remain active? And most importantly:

How do you find a balance between giving and taking?

From Experience: What (Not) To Do

  • Clear communication: From day one, rules and expectations should be clear. This prevents confusion and provides structure within the group.
  • Screening volunteers: It’s important to consider motivation and willingness to participate. Occasionally check in with volunteers you haven’t seen for a while to see if they still want to stay active. This way, space is created for new volunteers.
  • Membership fees: Are you an organization that works with paying members? Then it’s important that people continue to pay their fees to cover costs. If some members don’t pay but still use the facilities, it creates unfair situations. Make sure you also have rules for non-paying members and decide how your organization will handle that.

Step-By-Step Plan

  1. Step 1: Overview

    Make an overview of your active volunteers. Who are the active volunteers? What exactly do the active volunteers do for your organization? And what role do you play within the organization?

  2. Step 2: Rules and expectations

    Ensure that members know what is expected of them. When you are devising the rules, involve your volunteers/members in the process. This will give them a sense of ownership of the rules and make them important to them.

  3. Step 3: Consequences

    Make clear agreements with volunteers about their commitment. Also make clear agreements about what happens if they fail to meet their commitments.

  4. Step 4: Check in

    Schedule regular check-ins with your volunteers. This means asking your volunteers every so often how they feel things are going and whether they want to continue volunteering (and for how long).

  5. Step 5: Filtering

    Contact volunteers who have not been active for a while. Talk to them and try to find out why they are no longer active. Ask if they still want to be active and what is feasible for them in terms of commitment and time. Make clear agreements about this.

  6. Step 6: Team building

    Organize regular activities and meetings to strengthen the group spirit.

  7. Step 7: Appreciation

    Show appreciation for volunteers, recognize their efforts, and celebrate successes together. A compliment can mean a lot.
