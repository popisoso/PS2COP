How can I attract more members?
You can attract more members by being visible in the neighborhood, offering accessible activities, and collaborating with nearby organizations. Clear communication, word-of-mouth outreach, and partnering with community centers or schools can help you reach new groups. Regular events, open meetings, and a welcoming atmosphere encourage women to join and stay involved.
My organization moved locations.
If your organization has moved, inform the Krachtvrouwen in de Wijk network directly so your new location can be updated in shared materials, maps, and communication channels. Provide the new address, your updated opening hours, and whether the move changes the type of activities you offer. This helps other organizations, partners, and local women find you easily and ensures the network’s overview of neighborhood initiatives stays accurate.
