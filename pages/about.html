About Us

About Us // Our Project

Mission

Our mission is to support women's organizations in The Hague in their growth, professionalization, and self-reliance. We bring them together to share knowledge, experience, and practical insights, so that they are even better equipped to empower women from diverse backgrounds and promote equal opportunities.

Vision

We believe in the power of women's organizations as a driving force behind social change in the neighborhood. When these organizations connect with each other, a network is created that not only shares knowledge, but also jointly creates and reinforces solutions. In our vision, Kracht(vrouwen) in de wijk will grow into a sustainable platform that supports, inspires, and connects women's organizations—a place where their collective knowledge takes center stage and their impact becomes visible.

Main Project

The aim of Kracht(vrouwen) in de wijk is to strengthen women's organizations and groups in The Hague, with a focus on organizations that are active in vulnerable neighborhoods. By setting up and supervising a Community of Practice, we create a sustainable learning environment in which organizations exchange knowledge, collaborate on concrete solutions to shared challenges, and thus increase their social impact.

Core Values

1. Learning together

We believe that shared knowledge leads to strong organizations. That is why collective learning is central to our approach.

2. Connection

Women's organizations differ, but they also have a lot in common. By seeking connection, everyone becomes stronger.

3. Self-reliance

We support organizations so that they can move forward with their own knowledge, talents, and networks.

4. Recognition & visibility

We make the impact of women's organizations visible and value their essential role in the city.

5. Inclusivity

We work from the strength of diversity—cultural, organizational, and personal.
