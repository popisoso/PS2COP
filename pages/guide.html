Step-by-step guides: practical tools for women's organizations

Within Kracht(vrouwen) in de wijk women's organizations in The Hague have collaborated on five practical step-by-step plans. Each step-by-step plan is based on real questions and challenges from practice. They offer concrete tools, examples, and tips that are directly applicable to the work of women's organizations.

Whether you are looking for a location, want to grow as an organization, or want to create better cooperation within your team, these step-by-step plans will help you on your way.

Download the step-by-step plans below and use them to support your organization.

  • Guide 1:Finding a Location

    How to find an affordable location suitable for activities and your target group.

  • Guide 2:Organizational Structure

    The steps you need to follow when starting a new organization. Actions, considerations, and processes involved in setting it up successfully.

  • Guide 3:Preventing Internal Friction

    How to prevent friction within the organization and make sure everyone feels welcome.

  • Guide 4:Finding A Balance For Your Organization

    How to find the balance between giving and taking within your organization.

  • Guide 5:Uniting Different Generations

    How to connect different generations and bring them together.
