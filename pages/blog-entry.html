New Meeting Hub for Women in Laak

Jane Doe - January 1st 2025

Laak VrouwenBuurtLab Laak

The Women's Neighborhood Lab is located in the building of the former neighborhood and church center De Oase near the Wissel community center and was opened on February 4, 2022, by aldermen Arjen Kapteijns (Social Affairs and Employment) and Kavita Parbhudayal (Healthcare, Youth, and Public Health). The meeting center brings women from Laak together. Too many women still live in isolation, even in The Hague. That is why initiatives like this are so valuable: through activities and meetings, women are given the opportunity to break through their isolation and develop themselves further,” says Alderman Kapteijns (Social Affairs and Employment).
